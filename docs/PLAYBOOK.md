# DocFlow — The Playbook

**You do two things. DocFlow does the other forty.**

That's the whole system. If you remember nothing else, remember this:

> ### ④ Package Approved → ⑦ Send Package
> Two clicks. Everything else happens on its own, and the card tells you what's going on in plain English the whole way.

---

## The journey (10 steps)

Each hire moves through these on its own. **Bold = you do it. Everything else = automatic.**

| # | Step | What happens | Who |
|---|------|--------------|-----|
| 1 | **New candidate** | Marked "Hired" in the ATS → the card appears here by itself, role & pay filled in. | auto |
| 2 | **Waiting on details** | **Fill the hire fields** (name, email, title, supervisor, pay, start date). | 👤 you |
| 3 | **Letter built** | The offer letter writes itself in about a minute. Read it over. | auto |
| 4 | **Package Approved** | **Click it.** DocFlow builds the signing packet and shows you the exact email. Nothing sent yet. | 👤 you |
| 5 | **Packet built** | The signing packet is ready behind the scenes. | auto |
| 6 | **Ready to send** | You see the word-for-word email the candidate will get. Last check. | auto |
| 7 | **Send Package** | **Click it.** The candidate gets the email with their signing link + form. | 👤 you |
| 8 | **Signing** | The candidate signs on their phone or computer, one sitting. | candidate |
| 9 | **Signed & filed** | Signed copy archived, background check opened, everyone notified. | auto |
| 10 | **Done** | Signed ✓, form ✓, background clears → the hire is ready. | auto → 👤 |

## How to read the card

DocFlow narrates every step as a comment, in its own voice, always in this shape:

```
◆ DocFlow · 6 of 10 — Ready to send
──────────────────────────────
[what just happened, in plain words]

Over to you
   ✓ looks right → do this
   ✎ something off → do that
```

**The colors tell you who's up:**
- 🔵 **Blue / ⚙️** = the machine is working. Sit back.
- 🩷 **Pink / 👤** = your move. The card says exactly what.
- 🟢 **Green / 🎉** = done.
- 🔴 **Red / ❌** = something needs a look — the card explains what and how to fix it.

---

## The three gates to "Done"

A hire is truly done when **three things** finish (in any order):

1. ✍️ **Offer signed** (Adobe)
2. 📥 **New-hire form filled** (Monday form)
3. 🔎 **Background check clears** (tracked on the Background Checks board)

DocFlow waits for all three, then the card turns green. The candidate's "we got everything" email only goes out once signing **and** the form are both in.

---

## When something's off — you're never stuck

DocFlow never fails silently. If anything goes sideways, it says so and tells you the fix:

- **Candidate hasn't signed?** DocFlow nudges them for you (and reminds you if it drags).
- **Sent to the wrong info?** Select **✋ Revise**, fix the field, approve again — it rebuilds fresh and cancels the old one.
- **SharePoint copy didn't land?** It retries itself; or select **🔁 Retry SharePoint** to push it again.
- **Need to redo a step?** Just re-approve or re-send — DocFlow supersedes the old one cleanly.
- **Don't want reminders on someone?** Check **🔕 No Reminders** on the card.
